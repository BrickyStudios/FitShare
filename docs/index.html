Your workout.
As a sticker.

FitShare turns a Gadgetbridge workout into a transparent PNG for your Instagram story: distance, time, route, heart rate. Like Strava's stats sticker, without Strava and without an account.

Download the APKView on GitHub

Android 8.0 or newer. Free, no account, no internet permission. Get updates through Obtainium.

From watch to story in five taps

  1. In Gadgetbridge, open the workout and tap ⋮ → Share FIT File.
  2. Pick FitShare in the share sheet.
  3. No GPS in the file? Share the route from OpenTracks (KMZ or GPX) to FitShare too.
  4. Choose a layout, the stats and a colour, then tap Save PNG.
  5. In Instagram, open a story, tap Stickers → Photo and pick the PNG.

Step-by-step guide with tips →

Reads what Gadgetbridge exports

FIT files (sport, heart rate, zones, GPS if recorded) and the JSON details. Routes from GPX, KML or KMZ, so OpenTracks tracks work as they are.

Four overlay layouts

Stack, Row, Card and Map. Pick up to four stats in your order, white, black or the sport's colour.

Elevation, heart rate, splits

Elevation profile, heart rate graph, km splits with your fastest kilometre, heart rate zones.

Your look, one tap away

Save a setup as a named look. The last eight exports stay in a history, so the next workout looks like the last one.

Full stories and feed posts

Two finished designs, Night and Race bib, as a 9:16 story or a 4:5 feed post.

Stays on your phone

No account, no analytics, no internet permission. Files stay on your phone.

Questions

Is FitShare free?

Yes. It is open source under GPL-3.0, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need Strava?

No. FitShare reads the files Gadgetbridge exports and draws the sticker on your phone.

Which watches work?

Any watch Gadgetbridge syncs workouts from, for example Xiaomi Smart Band, Amazfit and Garmin. FitShare reads the FIT or JSON export, not the watch.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Gadgetbridge is Android-only, and so is FitShare (Android 8.0 or newer).

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. Install the APK from GitHub Releases, or add the repository to Obtainium for automatic updates.

Does my data leave the phone?

No. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload anything.
