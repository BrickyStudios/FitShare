Your workout.
As a sticker.

Tap Share in Mi Fitness, Garmin Connect, Zepp or Huawei Health, pick FitShare, and you get a transparent PNG for your Instagram story with distance, time and heart rate. Add the GPX and the route goes on it too. Like Strava's stats sticker, without Strava, an account or a subscription.

Download the APKView on GitHub

Android 8.0 or newer. Free, no account, no internet permission. Get updates through Obtainium.

From fitness app to story in five taps

  1. In your fitness app, open the workout and tap Share.
  2. Pick FitShare. It reads distance, time, heart rate, calories and elevation off the picture.
  3. For the map, export the workout as GPX or TCX and share that file to FitShare too.
  4. Choose a layout, the stats and a colour, then tap Save PNG.
  5. In Instagram, open a story, tap Stickers → Photo and pick the PNG.

Mi Fitness, Garmin Connect, Zepp, Huawei Health, Samsung Health, Strava: their share picture is enough. Also Gadgetbridge FIT and JSON, and routes as GPX, TCX, KML or KMZ.

Stack, Row, Card and Map. Pick up to four stats in your order, white, black or the sport's colour. Or a finished design, Night or Race bib, as a 9:16 story or 4:5 post.

Elevation profile, heart rate graph, km splits with your fastest kilometre, heart rate zones.

Questions

Is FitShare free?

Yes. It is open source under GPL-3.0, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Which apps work?

Any app whose Share button sends a picture of the workout: Mi Fitness, Garmin Connect, Zepp, Huawei Health, Samsung Health, Strava, Polar Flow, COROS and others. Gadgetbridge exports and GPX or TCX files work too. FitShare reads Latin script, so the app can be set to English, German, French, Spanish and similar languages.

Do I get the map from the share picture?

No, only the numbers. For the route, the elevation profile and the km splits, also share the workout's GPX or TCX file. Where each app hides its export.

Do I need a subscription?

No. FitShare is free, and so is every layout and graph in it. You don't need Strava, Garmin Connect+ or any other paid plan.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. FitShare is Android-only (Android 8.0 or newer).

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. Install the APK from GitHub Releases, or add the repository to Obtainium for automatic updates.

Does my data leave the phone?

No. Text recognition runs on the phone, and the app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload anything.
